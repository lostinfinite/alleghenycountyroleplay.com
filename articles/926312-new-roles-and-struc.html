New roles and changes to our structure.

ContentLTD Â· March 21, 2026

Since ACRP is now a registered LLC, there are some changes to our ranking structure.

Hey Everyone,

Recently, ACRP was registered as an official LLC within the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. To align with our legal structure, we are establishing an Executive Team with the following roles:

  • CEO — Chief Executive Officer
  • CFO — Chief Financial Officer
  • COO — Chief Operating Officer
  • CLO — Chief Legal Officer
  • CISO — Chief Information Security Officer
  • CDO — Chief Data Officer

Currently, the CLO and CISO positions remain unfilled. Until we bring on candidates for these roles, the Executive Team will cover their responsibilities alongside our existing operations.

Our three current leaders will transition to Executive positions:

  • ContentLTD: Director to CEO
  • Albion: Deputy Director to COO
  • Devin: Assistant Director to CFO

Later this year, we will open applications and interviews to fill the vacant Directive and Executive positions. New members will be selected through this process and voted on by the community.

This shift reflects both our regulatory obligations as an LLC and our evolution into a more structured organization. It does not change how you interact with ACRP day-to-day.

We're excited to formalize what we've built together. This structure gives us the foundation to scale thoughtfully, protect our community, and keep ACRP the place you want to be.

Thanks,
ContentLTD
