March Changelog — 3/12/26

ContentLTD · March 12, 2026

Bugs bugs bugs… and more bugs! So here's what we fixed.

Bugs

  • We fixed the Appeal process on the Banned Splash Screen.
  • We fixed a bug where punishments won't appear on the Dashboard/Home page.
  • And a few more related to accessibility.

What's New?

  • We added a Legal Request form for legal inquiries related to someone's data. This is found in Settings.
  • We redesigned Settings — added the app's version, legal documents, "Join our Community," and some accessibility settings.
  • Made the ban splash screen a little prettier.

For Staff

  • Added a lookup list to the Lookup tab so you don't have to manually enter a User ID.
  • Added an action bar at the bottom of Reports and Appeals to quickly take actions. This is still in the works — feedback is greatly appreciated for actions we can add.

What We Want to Add

  • Theme, Color Scheme, and enhanced accessibility settings.
  • The ability for staff to customize the action bar.
  • Add a global action bar and ditch the navbar at the top.
  • Add a suggestions form.
  • Add a form maker.

And more… We just need your help to figure out how to implement all of this!

Known Issues

There is a bug where things will unnecessarily pulse or invoke the pulsing animation. The current workaround is to enable Reduce Motion in Settings.

Bugs?

Email me or report it in the portal — Settings > Bug Reports. You can also reach me at contentltd@acrp.work.
