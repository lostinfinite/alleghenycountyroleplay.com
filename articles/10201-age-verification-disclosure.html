Age Verification Disclosure

ACRP Executive Team · March 29, 2026

This article covers how we verify your age and for what purposes.

Introduction

At Allegheny County Roleplay, we have a commitment to keeping our players safe, and in order to do that, we use a certain amount of information to determine how old you are and if you can play in our community. These age checks are very basic, and we use 2 different kinds. In this article, we will explain these kinds of age checks, why we do them, and what happens with your data.

Age Check Types

Age Fields

Age Fields are where we essentially ask for your age; in this case, we ask for your Date of Birth. This age check appears on the entry application, so chances are, you've already completed this. Your age is assessed automatically by our system to check if you are under 13. If you are under 13 based on your age, then you are automatically banned. We only get your Date of Birth if you are 13+, and this is only used for your application.

Facial Age Verification

If we have reason to believe that you misrepresented your Date of Birth, a staff member may flag your account for Facial Age Verification. The factors that may lead to this are:

  • How your voice sounds: If your voice is consistent with someone under 13, or if there are signs that you are artificially altering your voice.
  • How you conduct yourself in voice chat, in-game, or on Discord: If your behavior is consistent with someone under 13. This factor alone is not sufficient; it must be paired with at least one other indicator.
  • If you openly state that you are underage: This alone is a determining factor.

Important

Staff members are not permitted to request that you activate your webcam during any stage of this process. If a staff member makes this request, you should report them immediately.

How are Facial Age Verifications Conducted?

A staff member will either review a report on you or the behavior you exhibit around the staff member, and assess if they think that you may not be 13 years old. If a staff member determines that you are under 13, they will flag you for a Facial Age Verification session with our vendor Didit®. When you complete the session, an AI will determine how old you are.

You will have 30 days to contest your result if you are unhappy with the outcome. No human will see your Didit session or your face unless you contest it; in that case, an Executive member will review your session and act accordingly. You can find more information in our Privacy Policy.

This Didit® Session will collect features such as Liveliness Scores, IP Addresses, and so on to determine if you are real, if you are who you say you are, and to determine your age. All data collected is retained for 30 days; after 30 days, our vendor will automatically delete the Sessions. All that we store long-term is your age. That's it.

Note

This is a basic rundown of information we collect. You can see the full list in our Privacy Policy and Biometric Data Privacy Statement.

Appealing Age Bans

When you get banned for being underage, you will always have the option to appeal. If you appeal an age ban, you will 99% of the time be asked for a Didit® session to verify how old you actually are. If you already have a Didit® session and are contesting the result, say so in the appeal so someone can manually review it.

Closing Notes

We take the privacy of our members seriously. We recognize that not everyone is comfortable with facial data being processed by AI systems, and we have worked to limit collection to only what is necessary. If you have feedback on how these systems can be improved, contact us at ownership@acrp.work.

~ The ACRP Executive Team
