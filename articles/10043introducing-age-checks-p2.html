Introducing Age Checks (Phase 2)

The ACRP Team Â· February 9, 2026

Updates on ACRP's rollout of AI-powered age verification and age assurance, including new data practices and privacy details.

Hey Everyone,

We wanted to share some updates since our last article which introduced the basic concept of Age Verification. Since that announcement, we have been building systems to fully introduce Age Verification and Age Assurance measures.

What's Changing?

  • We are now using AI for age verification.
    This change has officially begun rolling out. All users who previously completed age checks via forms will now need to submit a face scan in order to retain access to ACRP's systems.
  • Age Assurance
    We are developing additional age assurance technologies to help ensure that a user is not under the age of 13. This system works by scanning user messages and comparing patterns against training data. More details will be released in the coming weeks.

Your Data

We are using Didit for Age Assurance and facial scans. We understand that some users may have concerns about this provider. At this time, Didit reports no known history of data breaches. You may review their privacy practices here: didit.me/terms/privacy-policy

We have also updated our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy to reflect our current and upcoming changes. For details on how we process and retain data, review them at alleghenycountyroleplay.com/privacy.

If you have questions or concerns about these policies, email us at ownership@acrp.work.

Why We're Doing This

We're making these changes to help ensure a safe environment for all, reduce the risk of underage users who may be vulnerable, and ensure compliance with Discord's policies.

A few footnotes

We understand that these changes may affect some users, and we value your feedback. We remain committed to putting users first, always.

Thank you for your continued support.
