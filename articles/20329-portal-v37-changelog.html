V3.7… The update of Legal and Improvements. Let's Talk.

ContentLTD Â· March 29, 2026

V3.7 is here — and it's a big one for the legal side of things. Correspondence tracking, a compliance tab, a redesigned Lookup tool, Permission Keys, and finally, Partnerships and Expenses are live.

Changes for our staff.

  • Added Legal Correspondence Tracking.
  • Redesigned the "Request your Data" system.
  • Added a Compliance tab to log data breaches and similar incidents.
  • Organized Reports and Appeals.
  • Combined all legal-related tools into one unified "Legal" tab.
  • Added better Shift tracking for Dynamic Stage Notifications.
  • Added the ability to ask a user for more information on a report.
  • Redesigned the Lookup tool to be more user-friendly.
  • Added an "Alts" tab so you can see alternate accounts a user may have.
  • Officially released the Partnerships tab.
  • Officially released the Expenses tab. (Our CFO will thank me later.)
  • Redesigned the Age Verification tab for the organization.
  • Added Permission Keys.
  • Added a Redact tool.
  • Added more Anti-Fraud flags to our Legal Request system.

Other stuff that is helpful for you.

  • More Dynamic Stage Notifications — speaking of, we added Dynamic Stage Notifications!
  • Redesigned the Sidebar… Again.
  • Moved our Entry Application to the portal.
  • Fixed over 100 logged instances where your chosen accent color wasn't consistent.
  • Added a "Display Name" function in User Preferences, so you can customize what the greeting says.
  • Redesigned the Data Center in Settings.
  • Added a new "Reports & Appeals" tab.
  • Added the ability to see your Reports and Appeals and their outcome.
  • Fixed a few Dynamic Stage bugs, specifically related to the spring animations.
  • Made Action Bar customization easier.

Shame List

(A.K.A. the bugs we know about and haven't fixed yet.)

  • Content Density is broken… Again.

Closing Notes
