March Changelog — 3/7/26

ContentLTD · March 7, 2026

This is weird… so here's this month's changes! Forgive us if you find something that's broken.

Security

Over the course of the last 7 days, we found and patched 108 major security vulnerabilities. You may find the legal disclosure here. Just know that your data is safe, though.

Accessibility

  • Added ARIA labels to most buttons, text, and interactive elements.
  • Added the ability to navigate the Portal via Tab.
  • Improved the layouts to help Screen Readers work independently of visual design.

QoL and Simple Design Wins

(Helps me sleep easier at night, anyhow.)

  • Made colors and typography more consistent across the portal.
  • Changed text sizes and weight across forms.
  • Fixed Input and Form Fields.
  • Improved buttons in terms of consistency and roundness.
  • Added micro-interactions, and improved interactions & motion in general.
  • Made UX better and finally make sense.
  • Made components more consistent.
  • Added Mobile and Tablet support.

Known Issues

  • CAPTCHAs may be excessive.
  • There may be oversized fonts and buttons on some splash screens.
  • The appeal button on the "Banned" splash screen is not working properly.

Bugs?

Email me or report it in the portal — Settings > Bug Reports. You can also reach me at contentltd@acrp.work.
