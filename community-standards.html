Community Standards

These Community Standards govern the conduct of every individual who participates in ACRP in any capacity, across every platform and system we operate. They are a component of ACRP’s broader legal framework and are expressly incorporated into the Terms of Service.

Last Updated: December 16, 2025
Effective: December 16, 2025
Established: December 16, 2025

Preamble

Allegheny County Roleplay (“ACRP”) is a Pennsylvania LLC operating a structured, serious Emergency Response: Liberty County (“ER:LC”) roleplay environment. These Community Standards (“Standards”) govern the conduct of every individual who participates in ACRP in any capacity, across every platform and system we operate, including but not limited to our Discord server, our Roblox private server, the ACRP Portal (portal.alleghenycountyroleplay.com), and any affiliated communication channel or service.

These Standards are a component of ACRP’s broader legal framework. They are intended to be read alongside, and are expressly incorporated into, ACRP’s Terms of Service (“Terms”). Defined terms used in these Standards that are not separately defined here carry the meanings assigned to them in the Terms. In any conflict between these Standards and the Terms, the Terms control.

Participation in ACRP is a privilege, not a right. Access to our community, platforms, and servers may be restricted or revoked at any time, with or without prior notice, in accordance with these Standards, the Terms, or any other governing documentation. No specific number of prior violations is required before permanent enforcement action is taken. No prior warning is guaranteed.

These Standards are intended to be read in full. Claiming ignorance of a rule is not a defense against enforcement.

If you have a question about whether a specific action is permitted, contact staff before proceeding. We would rather answer a question in advance than take enforcement action after the fact.

// Part I

Safety

ACRP is a roleplay community. Real-world emergencies are not roleplay, and ACRP’s internal reporting tools are not substitutes for emergency services.

If you witness or reasonably believe that any individual is in genuine danger due to a mental health crisis, a threat of physical violence, or any other life-threatening situation, you are required to contact real emergency services immediately. Do not wait for an ACRP staff member to respond before calling for help.

Required actions:

  • In the United States: dial 911 for physical emergencies or 988 for mental health crises
  • Outside the United States: contact the appropriate jurisdictional emergency services
  • If the individual is active in ACRP: notify ACRP staff in addition to contacting emergency services

As established in Terms §7b, by using our Services, you accept responsibility for initiating real-world assistance if someone is at risk. Failure to act in a situation where you had reasonable awareness of a genuine emergency may itself result in enforcement action at ACRP’s discretion.

ACRP will cooperate fully with law enforcement, emergency services, and relevant platform safety teams in any situation involving a credible threat to life, as described in Terms §7c. This cooperation may include disclosure of logs, account identifiers, and other data as permitted by law and our Privacy Policy.

As described in Terms §7d, ACRP operates a dedicated high-priority detection layer within its content classification infrastructure. This layer scans for content referencing mass casualties, bomb threats, active shooter situations, or explicit threats to harm named or general groups of individuals. It operates independently of, and with higher priority than, the general content classifier described in Part IV of these Standards.

When this layer activates, an escalated internal review workflow is triggered automatically. That workflow may result in the content being preserved, compiled into an evidence dossier, and referred to law enforcement or the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) following review by designated ACRP staff. No external submission occurs automatically. All referrals are subject to human review prior to transmission.

The following conduct is prohibited across all ACRP platforms at all times. These prohibitions are not subject to context, intent, or prior relationship between parties. A single violation of any item in this section may result in permanent removal without warning and may result in referral to law enforcement.

  • Threats of physical harm, violence, or death directed at any individual or group, whether inside or outside of ACRP
  • Sexual content involving minors in any form, including in roleplay contexts
  • Sharing, threatening to share, or soliciting another person’s private identifying information without their consent (doxxing)
  • Content that promotes, glorifies, or facilitates self-harm or suicide
  • Bomb threats, mass casualty threats, or any content referencing planned real-world violence
  • Impersonating law enforcement, emergency services, or ACRP staff in a manner intended to deceive
  • Any conduct that triggers a mandatory reporting obligation under applicable law, including content involving child sexual exploitation
// Part II

Respect

ACRP prohibits harassment, targeted bullying, hate speech, and discrimination in any form. This applies to conduct or content directed at any individual or group on the basis of:

  • Race, ethnicity, or national origin
  • Gender, gender identity, or gender expression
  • Sexual orientation
  • Religion or belief system
  • Disability status, including physical, cognitive, or mental health conditions
  • Age
  • Any other characteristic protected under applicable law

Harassment is defined as repeated or sustained conduct directed at a specific individual or group with the intent or effect of distressing, demeaning, intimidating, or excluding them. A single severe incident may also constitute harassment depending on its nature and context. ACRP staff will evaluate each situation using the totality of the circumstances.

Discriminatory content, slurs, and hate speech are prohibited regardless of whether they are directed at a specific individual. Using a slur in any context, including ironically or as part of a joke, is a violation of these Standards.

This section is consistent with and supplements the UGC prohibitions in Terms §8, which independently prohibit hate speech, racism, harassment, and threats across all ACRP Services.

The following conduct standards apply to all members in ACRP’s Discord server at all times.

General conduct:

  • Keep all content and conversation relevant to ACRP and appropriate to the channel in which it is posted
  • Do not spam, mass-ping, or otherwise deliberately disrupt channels or conversations
  • Do not post NSFW content of any kind in any channel
  • Do not self-promote, advertise other communities, or recruit members for external projects without explicit prior written approval from ACRP management
  • Follow all staff instructions promptly. If you believe a staff instruction was incorrect, use the appeals process described in Part IV. Do not argue with or ignore staff in active channels.

Channel usage:

Each channel in the ACRP Discord server exists for a specific purpose. Use channels for their intended purpose. Off-topic posting, cross-posting the same content across multiple channels, and using channels to circumvent or evade moderation actions are all violations of these Standards.

Direct Messages:

ACRP moderates direct messages where violations are reported by recipients. As stated in the Terms, sending unsolicited advertising, NSFW content, or content that violates our Terms of Service to another ACRP member through direct message is a violation of these Standards and may result in enforcement action.

Mentions and notifications:

Use @mentions thoughtfully and sparingly. Mass-pinging, pinging staff without cause, and abusing role mentions are prohibited.

Interpersonal disputes between members are to be handled through staff, not in public channels. If you have a conflict with another member, bring it to a staff member through private message or the appropriate support channel.

The following are not acceptable responses to interpersonal conflict and will themselves result in enforcement action:

  • Posting callouts, accusations, or complaints about another member in public channels
  • Recruiting other members to target, mock, or pressure a specific individual
  • Threatening to leak, expose, or publish private information about another member
  • Continuing a dispute in public after being directed by staff to take it private

ACRP reserves the right to take enforcement action against all parties involved in a public dispute, regardless of which party initiated it.

ACRP staff exercise authority on behalf of the organization. Members are expected to follow staff instructions during active moderation situations. The appropriate channel for disagreeing with a staff decision is the formal appeals process, not public argument, confrontation, or coordinated pressure.

The following are prohibited:

  • Arguing with, ignoring, or deliberately circumventing staff instructions
  • Publicly accusing staff of bias, misconduct, or bad faith without going through the formal complaint process
  • Threatening staff members personally, including threats to report them to external platforms or authorities without a legitimate factual basis
  • Attempting to coordinate other members against a specific staff member

Complaints about staff conduct may be submitted to management at ownership@acrp.work. Complaints submitted in good faith will be reviewed. Frivolous or retaliatory complaints may themselves result in enforcement action.

Integrity

The following rules apply to all participants in the ACRP ER:LC private server at all times, regardless of department, rank, or experience level.

3.1.1 Staff Authority

Staff decisions are final during active server sessions. All participants are expected to comply with staff instructions immediately. Disputes with a staff decision are resolved through the appeals process described in Part IV, not by challenging or ignoring staff in-server.

Management may take enforcement action at any time, including without following progressive discipline procedures, where the circumstances require it. The existence of an appeals process does not limit management’s authority to act immediately.

3.1.2 Alt Accounts

Alt accounts are prohibited. Participating in the ACRP server under any account other than your registered account requires explicit prior written approval from ACRP management. Using an alternate account to bypass any enforcement action, including a ban, restriction, or warning record, is a separate and independent violation that will result in permanent removal of all associated accounts. This is consistent with Terms §11c, which prohibits circumvention of any restriction through alternate accounts, proxies, or affiliated individuals.

3.1.3 No Intention to Roleplay (NITRP)

Joining the server to grief, troll, or disrupt without engaging in good-faith roleplay will result in removal. NITRP includes but is not limited to: random vehicle ramming, unprovoked attacks on other players, deliberately derailing active scenarios, and joining the server solely to obstruct or harass other participants.

3.1.4 Cheating and Exploits

Using any exploit, bug, script, or unauthorized modification to gain an unfair advantage is prohibited. This includes but is not limited to speed exploits, noclip, unauthorized teleportation, and any modification to server behavior not authorized by ACRP. Discovery of an exploit must be reported to staff. Exploiting a known bug rather than reporting it is itself a violation.

3.1.5 Weapons

All banned weapons are disabled server-side. Attempting to access, equip, or use a banned weapon through any means, including exploits or unauthorized third-party tools, is prohibited.

3.2.1 Random Deathmatch (RDM) and Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM)

Killing or seriously injuring another player without a valid, established roleplay reason is RDM and is prohibited. Ramming another vehicle without a valid roleplay reason is VDM and is prohibited. Claiming an indirect or tenuous roleplay justification does not excuse RDM or VDM. Both are enforced strictly.

3.2.2 New Life Rule (NLR)

After dying, being hospitalized, or being released from jail, your character has no memory of the events that led to that outcome. You may not:

  • Return to the scene of your character’s death during the same scenario
  • Continue pursuing the same individuals who caused your character’s death
  • Use information your character obtained before death to influence your actions after respawning

NLR applies until the active scenario has fully concluded or until staff explicitly releases you from it.

3.2.3 Fear RP

Fear RP is the requirement to act as if your character’s life has genuine value. If your character is outnumbered, restrained, or has a weapon aimed at them, you must act in accordance with that situation. Ignoring an active threat to your character’s life, attempting to flee when restrained, or acting invulnerable to danger are Fear RP violations.

3.2.4 Full Killing

After your character is downed, do not respawn until one of the following conditions is met:

  • EMS personnel have responded and completed their roleplay at your location
  • All suspects involved in the scenario have left the immediate area
  • Staff have explicitly authorized an early respawn

Respawning prematurely disrupts EMS roleplay and active scenarios and is a violation of these Standards.

3.2.5 Driving and Accidents

All participants are expected to drive realistically and in accordance with traffic patterns appropriate to the server environment. The following are prohibited:

  • Driving at excessive speeds through populated areas without a valid scenario reason
  • Using a vehicle as a weapon outside of an authorized scenario
  • Ignoring traffic signals, lane markers, or road conditions in a clearly non-realistic manner

Any collision at or above 30 mph must be roleplayed. This includes remaining at the scene, calling EMS if applicable, and engaging with responding units. Leaving the scene of a significant collision without roleplaying it is a violation.

3.2.6 Cop Baiting

Deliberately provoking law enforcement into initiating a pursuit or response without a legitimate roleplay basis is prohibited. This includes driving erratically near patrol units, committing minor violations solely to attract attention, and intentionally creating situations designed to generate law enforcement responses without a valid story reason.

3.2.7 Panic Button and Radio Abuse

Abusing the panic button, spamming radio channels, or using emergency communications systems for non-emergency purposes is prohibited. Radio communications must be used in good faith and in accordance with realistic radio protocols for your department or role.

3.2.8 Cuffing and Transport

Arrests must be fully roleplayed. Rush cuffing, skipping the verbal interaction phase of an arrest, and transporting a suspect without engaging in appropriate roleplay are prohibited. Law enforcement and EMS are expected to coordinate in accordance with realistic protocols.

3.2.9 Safe Zones

The following locations are designated safe zones. No hostile action, active pursuit, or major criminal scenario may originate at or within these locations:

  • Spawn areas
  • Police stations and substations
  • Fire stations
  • DOT stations and depots
  • The hospital and surrounding property

Engaging in hostile action within a safe zone, or deliberately using a safe zone to avoid the consequences of an ongoing scenario, is prohibited.

3.2.10 Interferences

Only engage in an active scenario if you were involved from its beginning. Inserting yourself into an ongoing pursuit, shooting, negotiation, or medical call that you were not a part of is interference and is prohibited. If you arrive at a scene and are uncertain whether you should be involved, wait for staff or the scenario participants to direct you.

3.2.11 Priorities and Major Scenarios

Major priority scenarios require prior staff approval before initiation. This includes:

  • Bank and jewelry store robberies
  • Hostage situations
  • High-speed pursuits initiated by players
  • Organized escort scenarios
  • Organized protests

All major priority requests must be submitted through the designated Priority Request channel and require a completed request form before approval will be granted. Initiating a major priority without approval may result in the scenario being terminated and enforcement action taken against the initiating parties. Major criminal scenarios require a minimum of three participants to initiate.

3.2.12 Protesting

Protest roleplay is permitted only under the following conditions:

  • Prior written approval from ACRP staff has been obtained before the protest begins
  • The protest remains non-violent throughout
  • The protest does not involve racial, political, or divisive real-world themes

Protests that do not meet all three conditions will be terminated by staff. Participants who initiated or continued an unapproved protest may face enforcement action.

The following roleplay scenarios are prohibited regardless of context, participant consent, or claimed story justification. These restrictions apply even where all participants agree and even where the scenario occurs in a private or limited channel.

  • Suicide RP: Any roleplay scenario that depicts, simulates, or glorifies suicide or self-harm in any form
  • Bomb RP: Any roleplay scenario involving bomb threats, explosive devices, or mass casualty events, unless explicitly approved in advance by ACRP management as a structured server event
  • Torture RP: Kidnap and hostage scenarios are permitted within the Priority rules, but torture is prohibited in all forms
  • Minor RP: Any roleplay that sexualizes, inappropriately depicts, or targets minors, consistent with Terms §1 and the absolute prohibitions in Part I of these Standards
  • Roblox ToS Violations: Any scenario that violates Roblox’s Terms of Use, Community Standards, or platform policies

Avatars: All participants must use realistic avatars appropriate for their in-server role. Civilian avatars must be plausible and appropriate in appearance. Fantasy, abstract, or clearly non-human avatars are not permitted.

Uniforms: Department members are required to wear approved department uniforms while operating in their department’s capacity. Wearing a department uniform without active membership in that department is prohibited.

Liveries: Only ACRP-approved custom liveries may be used on department vehicles. Unapproved modifications to vehicle appearance are not permitted. Civilians may use standard vehicle appearances and approved civilian liveries only.

Accountability

ACRP staff and management are authorized to take enforcement action against any member at any time for any violation of these Standards, the Terms, or any other governing documentation. Enforcement action does not require a minimum number of prior violations. Staff may act without following a progressive discipline sequence where the circumstances require it, including but not limited to:

  • Safety violations under Part I of these Standards
  • Violations of Terms §7 (Emergencies) or Terms §11 (Blacklisting and Cease and Desist Authority)
  • Ban, restriction, or account evasion of any kind
  • Severe or egregious single-incident misconduct
  • Conduct that poses a credible threat to the community, staff, or the integrity of ACRP’s systems

Management retains full authority to act in any situation, at any time, where management determines immediate action is necessary. As stated in Terms §1, ACRP Staff members may restrict your use of ACRP’s Platform, Services, and Servers at any time.

The following enforcement actions are available to ACRP staff and management. This list is not exhaustive. Actions may be applied individually or in combination, and may escalate or de-escalate based on a member’s history and the circumstances of each violation. ACRP does not guarantee that enforcement will follow any particular sequence.

Action Description
Verbal WarningAn informal correction issued during an active session. Not formally logged.
Formal WarningA logged warning applied to the member’s account record. Formal warnings accumulate and inform future enforcement decisions.
Temporary MuteRestriction of a member’s ability to send messages in Discord channels for a specified duration.
Server KickRemoval from the Roblox private server for the current session. Does not affect Discord access unless separately actioned.
Temporary SuspensionTemporary removal from the Discord server, Roblox server, or both, for a defined period.
Permanent BanPermanent removal from all ACRP platforms, systems, and affiliated networks.
BlacklistingApplied to external servers, communities, or operators under Terms §11a. Denies access to all ACRP-operated infrastructure, partnerships, and community resources.
Cease and DesistIssued under Terms §11b. Upon issuance, the recipient is immediately and automatically restricted from all ACRP Services, Platforms, and Servers for the duration specified, or permanently if no duration is stated.

A first-time violation may result in a permanent ban if the circumstances warrant it. No prior warning is guaranteed.

As described in Terms §8a, ACRP operates an automated content classification system that monitors messages in designated Discord channels in real time. This system runs entirely on ACRP’s own infrastructure. No message content is transmitted to any third-party AI provider for classification.

The classifier evaluates each message across eight content categories:

  1. Self-harm
  2. Directed harm
  3. Hate speech
  4. Harassment
  5. Sexual content
  6. Drugs
  7. Weapons
  8. Violence

The system uses a combination of a machine-learning model and a deterministic rule engine. The rule engine executes first and applies fixed enforcement responses for the most severe content patterns regardless of threshold configuration. The system also applies normalization techniques, including leetspeak detection, homoglyph detection, and phonetic substitution analysis, to prevent evasion attempts.

When a violation is detected, the message is removed and an automated Formal Warning is issued to the account. A notice is posted in the relevant channel for 15 seconds and includes citations to the applicable Terms and Standards sections, links to all relevant legal documents, and a direct link to the appeals portal. The affected member also receives a private message with full enforcement details. All automated actions may be appealed through the Portal.

Automated content moderation is also disclosed in the /info command footer in the ACRP Discord server, which references Terms §8a and Privacy Policy §4.

As described in Terms §10, ACRP requires age verification and age assurance checks as a condition of access to and continued use of our Services. Members must be at least 13 years old to access or use ACRP Services.

Verification measures include screening forms, AI-based facial age estimation and liveness checks performed by our verification provider Didit.me acting as a data processor, and behavioral and message-based age assurance signals. Completion of required verification is mandatory. Refusal or failure to complete verification will result in denied or removed access.

Accounts that fail verification, trigger underage risk signals, submit false information, or attempt to bypass verification controls are subject to suspension, removal, or permanent ban under Terms §10b. Attempting to bypass Age Proofpoint verification through any means is a serious violation of both these Standards and the Terms.

As established in Terms §11e, ACRP reserves the right to deny or revoke access to any individual based on their reputation or conduct within the broader ER:LC community, the Roblox platform, or any public-facing social media space, regardless of whether that conduct occurred within ACRP’s own systems.

Factors ACRP may consider include a pattern of harmful, deceptive, or bad-faith conduct in other communities; public statements or behavior incompatible with ACRP’s values; and association with servers, individuals, or organizations that ACRP has blacklisted or issued cease and desist notices to. ACRP is not required to disclose the specific basis for a reputation-based access decision.

ACRP reserves the right to conduct its own due diligence, including reviewing publicly available social media profiles, community records, and third-party reports, when evaluating whether to admit or retain a member. Access denials under this section are final and are excluded from the standard appeals process described in §4.6 below.

Members who believe an enforcement action was applied in error may submit a formal appeal through the ACRP Portal at portal.alleghenycountyroleplay.com.

Appeals process:

  1. Submit your appeal through the Portal within 30 days of the enforcement action
  2. Include a clear description of the action taken, why you believe it was applied in error, and any supporting information
  3. Your appeal will be reviewed by staff who were not involved in the original enforcement decision
  4. You will receive a written determination within 30 days of submission

Limitations:

  • Submitting an appeal does not suspend or pause the enforcement action during review
  • Appeals are not available for decisions made under Terms §11, including blacklisting decisions, cease and desist actions, and reputation-based access denials under Terms §11e, unless ACRP expressly agrees in writing to reconsider
  • Frivolous or bad-faith appeals may result in restriction of your ability to submit further appeals
  • Staff members involved in the original enforcement decision are excluded from the appeals review process

Age Requirements and Verification

You must be at least 13 years old to access or use any ACRP Service. This requirement is consistent with COPPA, Discord’s Terms of Service, and Roblox’s Terms of Use. If ACRP’s systems determine or reasonably indicate that you are under the minimum required age, your access will be denied or revoked.

Minors who use the Services with parental consent are subject to these Standards in full. Parents and legal guardians who permit a minor to access ACRP’s Services are subject to the Terms and are responsible for the minor’s conduct across all ACRP platforms, as stated in Terms §1.

As described in Terms §10a, ACRP’s age verification system (Age Proofpoint) may require:

  • Completion of a screening form including your date of birth, country of residence, and related eligibility information
  • A live facial scan for biometric age estimation and liveness detection, processed by our verification provider Didit.me acting as a data processor
  • Review of behavioral and message-based age assurance signals through ACRP’s automated systems

Completion of required verification is mandatory. Refusal or failure to complete verification will result in denied or removed access to ACRP systems. By submitting verification data including facial scans, you consent to biometric and eligibility processing for age screening, fraud prevention, safety enforcement, and compliance purposes, as detailed in our Privacy Policy.
