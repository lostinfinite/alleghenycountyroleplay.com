What we are

Built to protect.
Not to police.

The Safety Assurance Council gives ACRP community members a real seat at the table when it comes to user-safety policy. Our role is advisory - we surface concerns, propose solutions, and act as a check on decisions that affect everyone.

We exist because safety and privacy are not opposites. Every policy we touch is examined through both lenses, and we push back when either one is sacrificed for the convenience of the other.

Who qualifies

Eligibility requirements

Three requirements. All are firm. Meet them, and you have a place here.

Age & Verification

You must be at least 13 years old and have completed age verification through ACRP's systems. No exceptions.

US or UK Region

You must be based in the United States or United Kingdom, and willing to submit proof of your region when requested.

Community Standing

At least one month of membership in the ACRP Discord, with a clean moderation history and a positive reputation in the community.

What membership includes

Council scope and responsibilities

Membership carries access, expectations, and formal recognition inside the program.

Protected discussion access

Members are brought into internal conversations where safety concerns, policy drafts, and enforcement edge cases are reviewed.

Policy input responsibility

Council members are expected to review proposals carefully and give clear, reasoned input before decisions are finalized.

Formal role recognition

Accepted members receive the Safety Assurance Council designation so the program has visible accountability and clear ownership.

Structured onboarding

New members complete a short onboarding so expectations, confidentiality, and review procedures are consistent from day one.

How it works

The application process

1
Submit your application

When applications open, fill out a short form - basic details and a statement of interest. No essays required.

2
Review & response

Directorship reviews every application. You'll receive a clear yes or no - within a reasonable timeframe, not silence.

3
Onboarding & training

Accepted members go through a brief onboarding covering our processes, expectations, and how the Council operates day-to-day.

4
You're a Council member

Welcome in. Participate in discussions, review proposals, and make a real impact on how ACRP protects its community.
