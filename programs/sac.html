About the Safety Assurance Council

Purpose: The Safety Assurance Council helps ACRP Directorship make decisions to assure user safety without infringing on users' privacy.

Mission

The Safety Assurance Council was created to help Allegheny County Roleplay Directorship make policies, rules, and systems to protect users and keep them safe while not infringing on user privacy and rights.

Eligibility

  • You must be at least 13 years of age and age-verified through our systems.
  • You must be based in the US or UK and be willing to submit proof of region.
  • You must be a member of the ACRP main Discord server for at least 1 month with a positive moderation history and reputation within the community.
