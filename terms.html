ACRP operates an automated content classification system that scans messages posted in monitored Discord channels in real time. This system is hosted and operated entirely on ACRP’s own infrastructure; message content is not transmitted to any third-party AI provider.

The classifier evaluates each message across eight content categories: self-harm, directed harm, hate speech, harassment, sexual content, drugs, weapons, and violence. It uses a combination of a machine-learning classifier and a deterministic rule engine. The rule engine runs first and applies a hard floor for the most severe content patterns regardless of any threshold configuration. The classifier also applies normalization techniques, including detection of leetspeak, homoglyphs, and phonetic substitutions, to prevent bypass attempts.

When a message meets the threshold for a violation, or when a hard-rule pattern fires, the message is deleted and an automated Warning is issued to the user. These actions may occur without prior review by a human moderator. Users who believe an automated action was applied in error may appeal at portal.alleghenycountyroleplay.com.

Every automated enforcement action generates a public notice in the channel where the content was posted. That notice cites the applicable sections of these Terms and our Privacy Policy, includes links to all relevant legal documents, and provides a direct link to the appeals portal. The notice is removed automatically after 15 seconds. The user also receives a private direct message with the enforcement details.

Automated content classification is also disclosed in the /info command footer in our Discord server, which references this section and Privacy Policy §4.
