iii.What’s different

Three things actually different from the average server.

  1. The radio works like a real radio.

    Melonly CAD and Melonly Radio are wired into our patrols when staffing allows. Units check in by call sign. Dispatch holds the board. Calls get pended when nobody’s free — same as the real thing.

  2. Command structures are real.

    Sergeants run shifts. Lieutenants approve unusual moves. Chiefs answer for outcomes.

  3. People stick around.

    Most members aren’t here for one session. They’re here long enough that you’ll recognize their voices on the radio, remember which deputy backed you up last week, and know who’s good for a serious traffic stop.

v.Frequently asked

Things people ask before applying.

How old do I have to be?

Fourteen or older. See our age-verification disclosure for how that works.

What tools are used?

Discord for everything off-radio. Melonly CAD and Melonly Radio during patrols when available.

Is the community still active?

Yes — actively building. We’re seeking experienced department heads to help shape it.

Do I need experience?

No. New roleplayers and veterans are held to the same standard: take it seriously, treat people well.
