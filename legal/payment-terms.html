Preorder delivery timelines are estimates and are not guaranteed. ACRP reserves the right to delay, modify, or cancel a preordered product at any time. Once a preordered product has been fulfilled and access or delivery has occurred, the standard no-refund policy applies.

3c. Monthly Subscriptions

A monthly subscription is a recurring billing arrangement charged once per calendar month. Your billing date is established at the time of your initial purchase and recurs on or around the same date each month. Monthly subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled before the renewal date in accordance with Section 7 of these Payment Terms.

You are responsible for ensuring your payment method remains valid throughout any active subscription period. If a renewal charge fails, ACRP may retry the charge, suspend your access, or terminate your subscription in accordance with Section 10 of these Payment Terms.

3d. Yearly Subscriptions

A yearly subscription is a recurring billing arrangement charged once per calendar year at a single lump sum covering the full twelve-month period. The annual rate, which may reflect a discount compared to the equivalent monthly rate, is displayed at the point of purchase. Yearly subscriptions automatically renew at the end of each annual term unless cancelled before the renewal date in accordance with Section 7 of these Payment Terms.

Yearly subscriptions are paid in advance for the full term. No prorated refund is available for unused months within a paid annual period, except as required by applicable law or as described in Section 6 of these Payment Terms.

3e. Monthly Subscription with Annual Commitment

A monthly subscription with an annual commitment is billed on a monthly basis and carries a minimum commitment period of twelve (12) months. You will be charged each month at the stated commitment rate for the duration of the commitment period. The commitment rate may differ from the standard month-to-month rate.

Payments made under this plan type are treated as installments on a deferred payment obligation. By purchasing this plan, you acknowledge that ACRP is extending access to its services on the basis of your agreement to complete the full commitment period, and that the structure of this plan constitutes a deferred payment financing arrangement as described in Section 3h and Section 4.

End-of-Commitment Notice. Twenty (20) days before the commitment period ends, ACRP will send you an email advising that the commitment is approaching completion and outlining your three available options: (1) renewing for another annual commitment term, (2) converting to a standard month-to-month subscription, or (3) cancelling at the end of the commitment period. A follow-up reminder will be sent seven (7) days before the commitment ends. If you do not respond to either notice, ACRP will renew your subscription under an annual commitment at the then-current commitment rate, except where the original plan was offered as part of a temporary or seasonal discount, in which case ACRP reserves the right to cancel the subscription at the end of the commitment period with same-day notice rather than auto-renew at a rate that no longer applies.

Early Cancellation. If you wish to cancel before the commitment period ends, two options are available. First, you may continue paying your existing commitment installment schedule through the end of the commitment period, after which the subscription automatically cancels and access is revoked. Second, you may pay off the remaining months at the full standard month-to-month rate rather than the discounted commitment rate. The payoff calculation under this option excludes all discounts, promotional pricing, and offers that applied to the commitment plan. Access is revoked immediately upon completion of the payoff. ACRP will calculate and provide the payoff amount upon request to legal@acrp.work.

Payoff Financing. If the remaining payoff balance under the early cancellation option exceeds five hundred dollars ($500.00), ACRP will offer you the option to finance the payoff through Affirm. If the payoff balance is three thousand dollars ($3,000.00) or more, you may contact ACRP at legal@acrp.work to request a one-time good-faith balance reduction of fifty percent (50%) of the outstanding payoff amount. This reduction is not guaranteed, is granted at ACRP's sole discretion, may only be applied once per account across all commitment plans, and is forfeited if you initiate a chargeback or fail to make scheduled payments on any outstanding balance.

A monthly subscription with a custom commitment period operates on the same billing structure and terms as Section 3e, except that the minimum commitment period is not fixed at twelve months. The specific commitment duration is stated on the product page at the time of purchase and governs in place of the twelve-month default.

All end-of-commitment notice procedures, early cancellation options, payoff calculations, and payoff financing thresholds described in Section 3e apply equally to plans under this section. Default, Late Fees, Interest, and Collections

10a. Minimum Payment

For any subscription or commitment plan billed on a recurring basis, your minimum payment due for each billing period is your standard scheduled payment for that period. For example, if your commitment plan is priced at twenty dollars ($20.00) per month, your minimum due each month is twenty dollars ($20.00). 10b. Late Fee

A late fee of thirty-five dollars ($35.00) is charged each time ACRP or its payment processor attempts to charge your payment method and the charge fails to process for any reason attributable to the cardholder or the payment method, including insufficient funds, an expired card, or a frozen account. This fee applies to every failed charge attempt, including retry attempts on the same balance. Late fees are not waived for consecutive failed attempts within a single billing cycle.

10c. Default Interest

If you fail to pay the minimum amount due for any billing period and the balance remains unpaid, interest will accrue on the outstanding balance at an Annual Percentage Rate (APR) of 15.99%, equivalent to a periodic rate of approximately 1.3325% per month. Interest compounds monthly on the accumulated unpaid balance, beginning on the date the payment became due. Interest continues to accrue until the full outstanding balance, including principal, fees, and prior accrued interest, is paid in full.

A one-time discounted late processing fee of twenty-five dollars ($25.00) will be applied to the defaulted balance in lieu of the standard per-attempt fee described in Section 10b, provided the default is cured within the same billing cycle in which it occurred. If the default is not cured within that billing cycle, the per-attempt fee structure in Section 10b applies to all subsequent charge attempts on that balance.

10d. Collections and Access Termination

If you fail to pay the minimum balance due for three (3) consecutive months and the accumulated unpaid balance for those three months remains outstanding, ACRP will cancel your subscription, immediately revoke your access to all ACRP services, and refer the outstanding balance to a third-party collections agency. ACRP will provide written notice to the email address on file before referring the balance to collections.

Third-party collections agencies engaged by ACRP operate independently and are required to comply with the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act, 15 U.S.C. § 1692 et seq. ("FDCPA"). ACRP is not responsible for the conduct of any collections agency acting on its behalf, but ACRP will only engage agencies that represent they are FDCPA-compliant. Referral to collections does not extinguish or reduce your payment obligation to ACRP.

10e. FDCPA Notice

If your account is referred to a third-party collections agency, that agency is required by law to provide you with a written validation notice within five (5) days of first contact, stating: the amount of the debt, the name of the creditor to whom the debt is owed (Allegheny County Roleplay LLC), and your right to dispute the debt within thirty (30) days.
