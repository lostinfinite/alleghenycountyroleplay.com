Biometric Data Privacy Statement

This statement explains how ACRP collects, uses, stores, retains, and deletes biometric data when users complete facial age estimation and liveness verification as part of ACRP’s Age Proofpoint system.

Effective: March 4, 2026
// 00

Introduction

Allegheny County Roleplay (“ACRP”, “We”, “Us”, or “Our”) uses biometric technologies as part of its age verification and assurance systems. This statement explains how biometric data is collected, used, stored, retained, and deleted when users complete facial age estimation and liveness verification.

This document is subject to the laws of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. ACRP’s Services are intended for U.S. residents only. Users in states with biometric-specific legislation, including Illinois (BIPA, 740 ILCS 14), Texas (CUBI, Tex. Bus. & Com. Code §503.001), and Washington, acknowledge that Pennsylvania law governs their use of ACRP services.

// 01

What We Collect

We collect a variety of biometric data, including but not limited to:

  • Facial images from live scans
  • Biometric feature vectors and templates
  • Liveness signals
  • Similarity and duplicate face indicators
  • Age estimation outputs and scores
// 02

Why We Collect

We collect this data to accurately estimate your age so we can assess if you are old enough to use our services as outlined in our Terms of Service. This information is also collected to prevent fraud and verify a user’s age.

// 03

How Data is Collected

Data is collected only during verification via user-submitted live facial scans. This process is controlled and regulated by Didit. See Section 5 for information on Didit and their role in age estimation.

// 04

Consent and Legal Basis

Consent is obtained in multiple ways. When joining and interacting with our services, the user provides consent by agreeing to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. When a verification link is made and clicked by the user, there is a consent page that requires the user to check a box and then click the “Continue” button to proceed with age verification.

Verification is required for access to the services. If the user refuses to complete the verification process, they may not use our services. Likewise, if a user has a result that detects them as being under 13 years of age, they may not use the services offered by us.

We receive the following data fields for each verification session:

  • Overall verification decision status
  • Verification feature list used in the decision
  • Liveness check status
  • Liveness score
  • Liveness method type
  • Face quality score
  • Face luminance or lighting score
  • Liveness warnings or risk flags
  • Age estimation value or age range
  • Age estimation confidence indicator
  • Duplicate face detection flag
  • Duplicate face risk status
  • Prior match count
  • Face similarity scores against prior sessions
  • Match status per prior session
  • Blocklist hit flag
  • Prior matched session identifiers
  • Prior matched session numbers
  • Prior verification timestamps
  • Check or node identifiers
  • Verification timestamps
  • Session identifier
  • Session sequence number
  • Vendor media references for captured face image
  • Vendor media references for captured video, if collected
  • Structured warning and risk log entries tied to the session
  • URLs to the reference image used for verification
  • URLs to the matched images from prior sessions
// 07

Use Cases

We use all information described above to verify you are who you say you are, to help fight against fraud, and to verify your age for eligibility purposes in accordance with our Terms of Service.

// 08

Retention

We retain the information described in Section 6 for 30 days, regardless of the verification result. If a user challenges a verification result, we may retain the result for longer, and the result will be subject to human review.

If we receive a data deletion request from the user, we will delete all verification sessions (open, in review, or closed, regardless of status) associated with the user’s Discord User ID and face via the dashboard within Didit®.

We will delete appropriate verification data when legally required, such as via a valid deletion request, or when the retention period expires.

// 09

Data Sharing

All verification data is shared with Didit® automatically, as they are the vendor who processes verifications as outlined in Section 5. Authorized staff may access a user’s age group (<13, 13–17, or 18+) and verification completion status.

We will share all required data with law enforcement if required by court order or other legally compelling legal process.

// 10

Safeguards

We take your data seriously. Only our Director has access to databases containing verification data. Additional safeguards are maintained by Didit® on their infrastructure.

// 11

User Rights

Where applicable, users have the right to do any of the following unless restricted by a court order, applicable U.S. law, or our Terms of Service:

  • Request data deletion
  • Request to see what data has been collected
  • Request verification via an alternative method that does not involve biometric data

Please note that some of these options are at the discretion of Allegheny County Roleplay. The following are legal requirements and not subject to ACRP discretion:

  • Request data deletion
  • Request to see what data has been collected
// 12

Refusal Consequences

If a user refuses to complete verification in some form, their access to the services will be severely limited or revoked in some cases at our sole discretion.

// 13

Policy Updates

This policy will be updated from time to time. As a matter of practice and as obligated by applicable laws, we will attempt to notify all users of material updates via email.
