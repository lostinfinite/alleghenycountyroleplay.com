About the Community

Welcome to ACRP

Allegheny County Roleplay is a whitelisted ER:LC community founded in 2024, built around realism, professionalism, and a genuine passion for emergency services roleplay. We don't just hand out whitelists — we build trained, organized departments that run like real agencies.

Whether you want to patrol as Pittsburgh PD, respond as Fire & EMS, enforce highway law as PSP, or keep the roads moving as PennDOT — there's a role for you here.

Whitelist Requirements

What You Need

Meet these four requirements and you're eligible to apply. No prior experience needed.

14+

Age Requirement

You must be 14 years or older to join. Exceptions can be made on a case-by-case basis — reach out to staff.

🖥

Computer Required

You must play ER:LC on a PC or laptop and maintain at least 15 FPS. Mobile devices are not supported.

EN

Fluent English

Clear communication is critical during patrol and dispatch. You must be able to speak and understand fluent English.

🎙

Working Microphone

A working microphone is required. You'll be using Sonoran Radio and Discord voice during active roleplay sessions.

// NOTE
Discord is Required to Complete the Process

You can submit your application online ahead of time, but you must join our Discord server to complete whitelist processing. All coordination, updates, and department communications happen there.

Ready to Apply?

Submit your application online, then join our Discord to finish the process. Applications are reviewed by staff and processed in order received.

Apply Now Join Discord

After applying, join Discord and wait for staff. Processing times vary based on volume.
