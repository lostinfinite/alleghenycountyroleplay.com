i.About the Community

Allegheny County Roleplay is a whitelisted ER:LC community built around realism, professionalism, and a genuine passion for emergency-services roleplay.

We don't just hand out whitelists — we build trained, organized departments that operate like real agencies. Whether you want to patrol with Pittsburgh PD, respond as Fire & EMS, enforce highway law as PSP, or keep traffic moving with PennDOT, there is a seat for you here.

ii.The Entry Process

Four pathways, one community.

The entry process depends on what you want to do in the County. Pick your pathway below — civilians have the shortest path; sworn departments add training and a department-specific Discord.

Civilian Pathway·3 steps·~ 1–3 days

  1. Submit your application

    Fill out the civilian application on the ACRP Portal. We ask the basics — your age, your experience, and how you plan to roleplay. Civilians get the simplest entry path in the County.

    ~10 min

  2. Join the Discord

    Hop into our main Discord server. Announcements, session pings, and community chat all live there. You will not complete onboarding without it.

    Required

  3. Wait and get approved

    Staff reviews civilian applications in the order received. Most decisions land within a few days. Once you are approved, you are clear to roleplay as a civilian in any active session.

    ~1–3 days

Law Enforcement Pathway · Pittsburgh Police Department · 6 steps

  1. Submit your application

    Fill out the main ACRP application and select PBP as your intended department. Be thorough — sworn applications get a closer read than civ.

    ~15 min

  2. Join the Discord

    Join our main ACRP Discord server. This is where staff coordinate the rest of your onboarding — keep your DMs open.

    Required

  3. Wait and get approved

    Staff reviews your community-level application. Once you are cleared at the community level, you move to the department side of the process.

    ~1–3 days

  4. Join the department's Discord

    After community approval, you will be invited to the Pittsburgh Police Department Discord. Department-specific announcements, training schedules, and the chain of command live there.

    Same day

  5. Apply & train

    Submit the department application inside the PBP Discord, then sit your training session with a Field Training Officer. Expect radio protocol, traffic stops, use-of-force policy, and a ride-along checkout.

    ~1–2 hours

  6. Hit the streets

    You are sworn in. Suit up, check in on Discord, and run a shift. Your FTO or a supervisor will ride with you on your first patrol to answer questions.

    Go time

iii.Whitelist Requirements

What you need.

Meet these four and you are eligible to apply. No prior roleplay experience is necessary — we train you.

14+

Age requirement

You must be 14 years or older to join. Exceptions may be made case-by-case — reach out to staff if you are close.

Computer required

You must play ER:LC on a PC or laptop with at least 15 FPS. Mobile devices are not supported.

En

Fluent English

Clear comms are critical during patrol and dispatch. You must speak and understand fluent English.

Working microphone

A working microphone is required. You will be on Melonly Radio and Discord voice during active sessions.

v.Common Questions

Frequently asked.

How long does the whole process take?

Most applicants are reviewed within one to three days. From submission to your first shift, plan on roughly three to five days depending on staff availability and how quickly you complete onboarding.

Do I need prior roleplay experience?

No. We train you. As long as you meet the four requirements and bring the right attitude, we will get you up to speed during onboarding.

What if I'm under 14?

Exceptions are handled case-by-case. Reach out to staff in our Discord — maturity and communication weigh more than the number alone.

Can I switch departments later?

Yes. Once you have put in some time at your starting department, you can apply for transfers or hold dual roles depending on staffing needs.

Is there a cost to join?

No. Joining ACRP is free. You will need ER:LC on PC, a microphone, and Discord — that is it.
