2. Proving Your Age

ACRP requires mandatory age verification and age assurance screening as a condition of access to its services. These measures are designed to prevent access by children under the age of 13 and to support compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and other applicable laws and platform policies. Access to ACRP systems is not permitted unless verification is completed. Users who attempt to circumvent or bypass these requirements will be permanently denied access.

We use a third-party provider, Didit.me, to perform AI-based age estimation and biometric liveness checks using facial data.

By proceeding with age verification, you provide informed consent to the collection, processing, and retention of biometric and verification data for eligibility screening and enforcement purposes. This consent is a condition of access and cannot be withdrawn while maintaining an active account. Withdrawal of consent requires deletion of your verification data and account, as described in section 8.

Verification may occur through the following methods:

a. AI-Based Facial Age Estimation and Liveness Detection

Users may be required to submit a live facial scan. Didit.me processes this scan using biometric analysis and liveness detection technology to estimate age and detect spoofing or impersonation attempts.

Processing may include:

  • Facial image capture
  • Biometric feature extraction
  • Age estimation scoring
  • Liveness detection scoring
  • Face quality and luminance metrics
  • Similarity and duplicate face detection results
  • Session and verification metadata

ACRP receives and retains verification results and related metadata, which may include estimation scores, liveness scores, match indicators, session identifiers, and associated technical logs. Didit processes facial images and biometric data as our verification vendor under their applicable terms and privacy policy. ACRP does not receive or store raw facial images beyond what is transmitted to Didit for processing.

Facial scan data and biometric scores are classified as sensitive data. Access is restricted to Authorized Staff under role-based controls. Authorized Staff may access this data only for the purposes of verification review, appeals processing, fraud investigation, or compliance with a valid legal request under U.S. law. All staff access is logged and subject to internal audit.

b. Screening Questions

Users may also be required to provide screening information such as Date of Birth, country of residence, and grade level. This information is used to assess eligibility and detect inconsistent or fraudulent claims. We may retain submitted values, derived age group, risk signals, and verification outcomes. Inconsistencies between screening answers and other verification data may be flagged for manual review by Authorized Staff.

c. ID Verification

Where Didit's AI-based age estimation returns a result near a defined age threshold, or where the estimated age falls below a minimum confidence margin, ACRP may require you to submit a government-issued ID for manual age verification. This step is triggered automatically based on Didit's scoring output and is not applied universally to all users.

Acceptable forms of ID are limited to the following:

  • Driver's License
  • Non-Driving ID / State-Issued ID
  • Birth Certificate

ID documents are collected and processed at the point where Didit makes an age determination. Authorized Staff may access submitted ID documents under the same role-based access controls that apply to facial scan data. All access is restricted on a need-to-know basis and is subject to logging and audit. No staff member may access verification data outside of an approved purpose.

Submitted ID documents are subject to the same 30-day retention period described in section e, unless an active review or appeal extends that period. Information derived from your ID, including your full legal name, Date of Birth, and age group, is used to construct a verification profile associated with your account. This profile is used to establish your identity and confirm your age for the purposes described in this policy. Where available and applicable, ACRP may cross-reference submitted information against records from your country of residence to validate the accuracy of submitted documents.

Verification data, including facial scan results and ID-derived information, is used for fraud prevention. This includes detecting duplicate or fraudulent accounts, identifying inconsistencies between submitted documents and prior verification records, and maintaining the integrity of ACRP's access controls.

Verification data collected through facial scans and ID submission may also be used, in whole or in part, to credential-verify the identity of a user in connection with law enforcement requests, legal process, or authorized user data requests. Such disclosure will only occur in response to a valid legal process directed to ACRP under U.S. law. ACRP will not voluntarily disclose verification data to any law enforcement or government authority outside the United States without a valid U.S. court order or equivalent legal instrument recognized under U.S. law.

Refusal to submit a required ID where verification is inconclusive will result in denial of access to ACRP services.

d. Use and Enforcement

Age verification and age assurance are used strictly to:

  • Determine whether a user meets minimum age requirements
  • Detect underage or high-risk accounts
  • Enforce platform access restrictions
  • Support moderation, safety, and compliance actions
  • Construct verification profiles for fraud prevention and identity confirmation
  • Support credential verification for valid legal process under U.S. law

If verification determines or reasonably indicates that a user is under 13, access to ACRP services will be denied immediately. Biometric and verification data associated with that determination will be handled in accordance with our verification data retention policy and may be retained for up to 30 days if a user requests a review or appeal. Users denied access will retain access to our portal solely for the purpose of submitting appeals or reviewing their verification data.

Attempting to bypass, falsify, or interfere with verification systems, including submitting fraudulent documents or impersonation attempts, constitutes a material breach of ACRP's Terms of Service and will result in permanent removal and may result in referral to appropriate authorities.

e. Data Retention

Verification records, results, scores, logs, and related metadata are retained for 30 days from the date of verification, unless a user requests a review of their verification determination, in which case the data will be retained for the duration of that review and will be examined by Authorized Staff.

If verification data is deleted upon user request, the associated verification status is also removed. Users who delete their verification data must complete the verification process again before regaining access to ACRP systems. Deletion of verification data does not exempt a user from enforcement actions already taken on the basis of that data.

Data retained beyond the standard 30-day period due to an active review will be deleted within 14 days of the conclusion of that review, unless further retention is required to comply with a valid legal obligation under U.S. law.

f. Third-Party Processing

Didit.me acts as ACRP's verification and biometric processing provider. Biometric capture and age estimation processing occur through their systems under a data processing agreement. Their privacy practices and security controls are governed by their published policies. Users acknowledge that biometric and verification data is processed by this provider as part of the screening workflow. ACRP has no control over Didit's internal processing operations beyond the terms of our agreement with them.

g. Security and Incident Response

Verification and screening data are stored and transmitted using industry-standard security safeguards, including encryption at rest and in transit. In the event of a confirmed data security incident affecting verification data, ACRP will assess the scope and nature of the incident and follow all applicable legal notification and mitigation requirements under U.S. law.

Where a breach affects 500 or more Pennsylvania residents, ACRP will provide concurrent notice to the Pennsylvania Attorney General in accordance with 73 P.S. Â§2305. Affected users will be notified as required by applicable law and, where practicable, by direct communication to the contact information on file.

ACRP will not delay breach notification to affected users at the request of any foreign government or regulatory authority. Incident response is governed exclusively by U.S. law.

Verification data is not sold. It is shared only with contracted processors and service providers as necessary to operate verification, safety, and compliance systems, or as required by valid legal process under U.S. law.

h. Requirement for Use

Completion of age verification and age assurance checks is a mandatory condition of access to ACRP services. Refusal to complete the required verification will result in denial or removal of access. ACRP reserves the right to re-verify users at any time if there is reasonable cause to question the accuracy of a prior verification.
