Introduction

Welcome to the Allegheny County Roleplay Privacy Policy. This document outlines how we (“we”, “us”, “ACRP”, “Allegheny County Roleplay”) will use users' (“users”, “you” or “your”) information. We do not knowingly collect data from users who are under the age of 13. If you are under the age of 13 then we ask that you do not use our services in accordance with our Terms of Service and Community Standards. This document is subject to the laws of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. ACRP’s Services are intended for U.S residents only. Users in states with biometric-specific legislation, including Illinois (BIPA, 740 ILCS 14), Texas (CUBI, Tex. Bus. & Com. Code §503.001), and Washington, acknowledge that Pennsylvania law governs their use of ACRP services.

Before moving on, we use certain phrases and words that we need to define.

  • “Minor” refers to any individual between the ages of 13 - 17
  • “Adult”, “Parent”, or “Guardian” refers to the parent or caretaker of any minor. Someone who has sole or major responsibility or custody of the minor.
  • “Identifying Documents” is any document used to identify you as an individual; this can be anything from a username to an ID, driver's License, or Birth certificate.
  • “ID” refers to any identification or driver's license that includes a Date of Birth and a Full legal name.
  • “Private Server” refers to any in-game or Discord® server owned, operated by, or maintained by Allegheny County Roleplay.
  • “Platform” refers to any feature and service, including websites, bots, applications, Private Servers, moderation tools, content, and custom functionalities offered by ACRP.
  • “Privacy Policy” refers to this document.

Personally Identifiable Information

We do not knowingly collect Personally Identifiable Information (“PII”) outside of official means, which can include our Appeal’s system and any other functionality or system used to collect any sort of information on you, including your email address, Discord® username, and so forth. We do not sell or share your Personally Identifiable Information (“PII”) for marketing purposes. We may share information with service providers that help operate our platform, including Mailgun for email delivery, Clerk for authentication and account infrastructure, and Didit for age verification services. Information may also be disclosed if required by law or in response to a valid law enforcement request. These third-party providers process certain data on our behalf and have their own privacy practices, which we encourage you to review.

For information on how we protect your data, please review section 6.

Proving Your Age

ACRP requires mandatory age verification and age assurance screening as a condition of access to its services. These measures are designed to prevent access by children under the age of 13 and to support compliance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA, and other applicable laws and platform policies. Access to ACRP systems is not permitted unless verification is completed.

We use a third-party provider, Didit.me, to perform AI-based age estimation and biometric liveness checks using facial data.

By proceeding with age verification, you consent to the collection and processing of biometric and verification data for eligibility screening and enforcement purposes.

Verification may occur through the following methods:

a. AI Based Facial Age Estimation and Liveness Detection

Users may be required to submit a live facial scan. Didit.me processes this scan using biometric analysis and liveness detection technology to estimate age and detect spoofing or impersonation attempts.

Processing may include:

  • Facial image capture
  • Biometric feature extraction
  • Age estimation scoring
  • Liveness detection scoring
  • Face quality and luminance metrics
  • Similarity and duplicate face detection results
  • Session and verification metadata

ACRP receives and retains verification results and related metadata, which may include estimation scores, liveness scores, match indicators, session identifiers, and associated technical logs. Didit processes facial images and biometric data as our verification vendor under their applicable terms and privacy policy.

b. Screening Questions

Users may also be required to provide screening information such as Date of Birth, country of residence, and grade level. This information is used to assess eligibility and detect inconsistent or fraudulent claims. We may retain submitted values, derived age group, risk signals, and verification outcomes.

c. Use and Enforcement

Age verification and age assurance are used strictly to:

  • Determine whether a user meets minimum age requirements
  • Detect underage or high-risk accounts
  • Enforce platform access restrictions
  • Support moderation, safety, and compliance actions

If verification determines or reasonably indicates that a user is under 13, access to ACRP services will be denied. Biometric and verification data associated with that determination will be handled in accordance with our standard verification data retention policy and may be retained for up to 30 days if a user requests a review or appeal of the determination. Users may be restricted from community platforms, including Discord servers and connected services, but will retain access to our portal for the purpose of submitting appeals or reviewing their verification data.

Attempting to bypass, falsify, or interfere with verification systems may result in permanent removal.

d. Data Retention

Verification records, results, scores, logs, and related metadata are retained for 30 days, unless a user requests a review of their verification, in which case the verification may be retained for longer and will be reviewed by a human.

If verification data is deleted upon request, the prior verification status is also removed. Users who delete their verification data must complete the verification process again before regaining access to ACRP systems. Deleted records do not exempt a user from enforcement actions already taken.

e. Third Party Processing

Didit.me acts as a verification and biometric processing provider. Biometric capture and age estimation processing occur through their systems. Their privacy practices and security controls are governed by their published policies. Users acknowledge that biometric and verification data is processed by this provider as part of the screening workflow.

f. Security and Incident Response

Verification and screening data are stored and transmitted using industry-standard security safeguards. In the event of a confirmed data security incident affecting verification data, ACRP will follow applicable legal notification and mitigation requirements. Where a breach affects 500 or more Pennsylvania residents, ACRP will provide concurrent notice to the Pennsylvania Attorney General in accordance with 73 P.S §2305

Verification data is not sold. It is shared only with contracted processors and service providers as necessary to operate verification, safety, and compliance systems.

g. Requirement for Use

Completion of age verification and age assurance checks is required to access and continue using ACRP services. Refusal to complete the required verification will result in denial or removal of access.

We conduct periodic reviews of our security practices. No system can guarantee absolute security, and we do not represent otherwise.
