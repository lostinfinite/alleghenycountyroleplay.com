Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Allegheny County Roleplay collects, uses, and shares information about you when you access or use our Services. It also explains your choices and rights regarding your data.

Introduction

Allegheny County Roleplay and its designated departments and staff team (collectively, “ACRP,” “Allegheny County Roleplay,” “we,” or “us”) offer the Allegheny County Roleplay website (www.alleghenycountyroleplay.com and www.acrp.work) and its associated platform, as well as various features, tools, bots, applications, content, in-game server, and custom functionalities (together, the “Services”) to allow users (“Users,” “you,” or “your”) to participate in our Emergency Response; Liberty County (“ER:LC”) environments and server(s).

This Privacy Policy describes how Allegheny County Roleplay collects, uses, retains, and shares information about you when you access or use our Services, Platforms, or Servers. It also describes your choices and rights regarding your data.

By using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, do not use the Services.

Operator Information

The Services are operated by Allegheny County Roleplay, an unincorporated community organization. For privacy inquiries, data requests, or legal correspondence, contact us at legal@acrp.work.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when you interact with our Services. This includes: Discord user ID, username, and discriminator; Roblox user ID and username; date of birth (where provided for age verification); country of residence; messages and communications sent through ACRP-operated channels; reports, appeals, forms, and other submissions you complete; and payment information processed through third-party payment processors (we do not directly store payment card numbers).

When you interact with our Services, our systems may automatically collect: log data including IP addresses, timestamps, and request information; device and browser metadata when you access our websites; moderation and enforcement records, including warnings, bans, and appeals; in-server activity records; and content classification signals from our automated systems, as further described in Section 4.

We may receive information about you from third parties including: Discord and Roblox (such as linked account identifiers); our age verification providers (such as Didit.me), who provide verification results, scores, and risk flags; payment processors who provide transaction status and fraud signals; and community members who report potentially harmful content or conduct.

Age Verification and Biometric Data

ACRP collects and processes age-related data for the purpose of verifying that users meet minimum age requirements under applicable law and platform policy. The types of data collected and how they are processed depend on the specific verification pathway used.

ACRP’s entry process may require you to submit a screening form that collects your date of birth, country of residence, grade level, and related eligibility information. This form data is processed by ACRP directly and is used to determine eligibility for access. Form data is retained in accordance with the retention schedule in Section 6.

Where ACRP requires biometric age verification, we use Didit.me, a third-party verification provider that processes facial images to estimate age and verify liveness. Didit.me acts as a data processor on our behalf. The following data is collected and processed as part of this flow:

  • Live facial image(s) captured during the verification session
  • Biometric features derived from facial geometry used for age estimation
  • Liveness detection signals used to prevent spoofing or impersonation
  • Estimated age range or pass/fail result
  • Device and session metadata (e.g., IP address, timestamp, browser/device identifiers)

Facial images are processed by Didit.me and are not permanently stored by Didit.me or ACRP after the verification session completes, unless a fraud or safety issue requires retention for investigation purposes. ACRP receives a verification result and associated metadata from Didit.me. ACRP does not receive raw facial images.

ACRP retains the following data from the verification process: verification status (pass/fail/flagged); age range estimate or eligibility score; fraud or anomaly flags; timestamp of verification; and associated Discord user ID. ACRP does not retain raw facial images or biometric feature templates.

Processing of biometric data for age verification is carried out on the basis of your consent (where applicable under state or national law), our legitimate interest in maintaining a safe and age-compliant community, and applicable legal obligations under COPPA and other child safety regulations.

If you are a resident of Illinois or Texas, additional protections may apply to the collection and processing of your biometric data under the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA) or the Texas Capture or Use of Biometric Identifier Act (CUBI). For full details, including disclosure of the purpose and length of collection, please review our Biometric Data Statement.

In addition to active verification processes, ACRP may use automated systems to analyze account activity and message patterns for signals that are correlated with underage users. These signals are used for risk assessment and may trigger a manual review or additional verification requirements. This analysis is conducted using ACRP-operated systems and does not involve transmission of content to third-party AI providers.

Verification results, scores, flags, and audit data retained by ACRP are generally kept for 30 days from the date of verification. Verified status may be retained for the duration of your active access to ACRP Services. Fraud and anomaly flags may be retained longer for safety and repeat-offender purposes. Screening form data may be retained for up to 90 days for audit and compliance purposes.

You may request deletion of your verification data by contacting us at legal@acrp.work. Deletion requests are subject to review and may be limited where retention is required for safety, fraud prevention, or compliance purposes. If verification data is deleted, your verification status is voided and you must re-verify before regaining access. Deletion of verification data does not reverse enforcement actions already taken.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to: operate, maintain, and improve the Services; verify your identity and age eligibility; enforce our Terms of Service, Community Standards, and other policies; detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and harmful conduct; respond to your requests, reports, and appeals; provide customer and moderation support; comply with applicable laws and regulations; maintain safety and security; process payments and manage subscriptions; and communicate with you about the Services, updates, and enforcement actions.

// 04

Automated Processing and Content Classification

ACRP operates an automated content classification system that analyzes messages posted in monitored channels. This system processes the text content of messages in real time. It is hosted entirely on ACRP-operated infrastructure. Message content is not transmitted to any third-party AI provider.

The system evaluates messages for eight content categories: self-harm, directed harm, hate speech, harassment, sexual content, drugs, weapons, and violence. It applies a combination of a machine-learning classifier and a deterministic rule engine. The rule engine runs first and can independently trigger enforcement for the most severe content patterns regardless of classifier output.

When content crosses a threshold or a hard-rule pattern fires, the message is deleted and an automated Warning is issued. These actions may occur without prior human review. Users may appeal automated enforcement actions at portal.alleghenycountyroleplay.com.

A separate, higher-priority safety layer runs independently of the general content classifier. This layer scans for content that may indicate potential imminent physical harm, including language referencing mass shootings, bomb threats, active shooter situations, or explicit threats to kill or attack individuals or groups.

When triggered, this layer initiates an escalated internal review workflow. As part of that workflow, relevant content may be compiled into an evidence dossier and, following human review by designated ACRP staff, may be referred to law enforcement or the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC). No external submission occurs automatically; all referrals require human review prior to transmission.

The use of automated content classification is disclosed to members of our Discord server via the /info command footer, which references Terms of Service §8 and this Privacy Policy §4. Every automated enforcement action also generates a public notice in the channel where it occurred, containing links to relevant legal documents and the appeals portal. That notice auto-deletes after 15 seconds. The affected user also receives a private direct message with enforcement details.

Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

Service providers and data processors: We share data with vendors and processors who help us operate the Services, such as Didit.me (age verification), Cloudflare (hosting and security), and payment processors. These parties are contractually restricted to processing your data only as directed by us.

Legal compliance and safety: We may disclose your information to law enforcement, regulatory agencies, or emergency services where we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to comply with a legal obligation, protect the rights or safety of any person, prevent or investigate fraud or abuse, or respond to a government request.

Child safety organizations: As described in Section 4b, content that may indicate imminent physical harm may, following human review, be referred to law enforcement or NCMEC.

Moderation and enforcement staff: ACRP staff members who are responsible for moderation and enforcement may access user records, logs, and communications as necessary to carry out enforcement actions.

Business transfers: If ACRP is dissolved, transferred, or merged, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to continued compliance with this Privacy Policy.

// 06

Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. The following general schedules apply:

  • Account and identity data (Discord ID, Roblox ID, linked account information): retained while you are an active member and for a reasonable period after account closure or removal.
  • Screening form data: up to 90 days from submission, unless a safety or compliance issue requires longer retention.
  • Verification results and flags: up to 30 days from the verification date, unless a fraud or safety flag requires longer retention.
  • Moderation and enforcement records: retained indefinitely for safety, compliance, and appeal purposes.
  • Message content and logs: retained for the operational period of the channel or bot, and may be retained longer if associated with an enforcement action or safety investigation.
  • Payment records: as required by applicable law and our payment processors’ requirements.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data, subject to legal and safety limitations described in this policy.
  • Restriction: Request that we restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format where technically feasible.
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interest.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at legal@acrp.work. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and no later than the period required by applicable law. We may require identity verification before processing your request. Some requests may be limited where retention is required for safety, legal compliance, or fraud prevention.

California residents may have additional rights under the CCPA/CPRA. Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, and other state residents may have additional rights under their respective state privacy laws. Contact us at legal@acrp.work to exercise any applicable state-specific rights.

You may configure your browser to block or delete cookies; however, doing so may affect the functionality of our web services.

Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. These measures include access controls for staff, use of encrypted communications, and hosting through reputable providers such as Cloudflare.

No security system is impenetrable. We cannot guarantee the security of information transmitted through third-party platforms such as Discord or Roblox, which are subject to their own security practices. If you become aware of a security vulnerability affecting our Services, please contact us promptly at legal@acrp.work.

Children’s Privacy

Our Services are not directed to children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Access to ACRP Services requires users to be at least 13 years old.

We use age verification and age assurance systems described in Section 2 to help identify and remove underage users. If we determine that a user is under 13, we will deny or revoke their access and delete any personal data we have collected from them, to the extent practicable and subject to any applicable safety or legal retention requirements.

If you believe we have collected data from a child under 13 without appropriate consent, please contact us at legal@acrp.work and we will take prompt corrective action.
